Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about reserving venues, 3D layouts, owner approvals, advance payments and security.

Why is Manual Owner Approval mandatory on Hall Khoj Finder? ▼
Indian wedding dates involve intricate planning, multi-day rituals, and catering coordination. To protect families from catastrophic double bookings or unconfirmed slots, Hall Khoj Finder strictly mandates owner manual approval. No booking is ever confirmed automatically until the venue owner personally accepts your event date and slot requirements.
When do I pay the advance token money? ▼
You only pay the token advance (typically 25% to 30%) AFTER the venue owner accepts your booking request. When the status changes to APPROVED, an online payment link is unlocked in your Customer Dashboard. You can pay securely via Razorpay (UPI, GPay, PhonePe, Cards, NetBanking).
How does the 3D Venue Walkthrough work? ▼
Our 3D engine is powered by WebGL and Three.js. You can rotate 360 degrees, zoom in, and toggle between Daylight, Sunset, and Night Gala lighting modes. It visualizes the elevated mandap stage, round banquet tables, and chandeliers. If your device or connection is low-bandwidth, the system automatically falls back to high-resolution 2D photo galleries.
Can I cancel my booking request if my plans change? ▼
Yes. Prior to owner approval, you can cancel your request instantly with zero charges from your Customer Dashboard. Once approved and confirmed with an advance token, cancellation and refunds follow the venue's stated cancellation policy visible on their detail page.
How do venue owners list their marriage halls or services? ▼
Venue and vendor owners can click "List Your Venue" or register as an Owner. You will be prompted to provide hall dimensions, capacity, photos, pricing packages, and GST/KYC verification documents. Once verified by our superadmin team, your venue goes live across India!
