Privacy Policy

Welcome to Hall Khoj Finder ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Information Technology Act of India and prevailing data protection principles.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information necessary to facilitate event venue discovery, 3D visualization, date booking requests, and secure payment processing. This includes:

  • Identity & Contact Data: Full name, mobile number, email address, and residential city.
  • Booking & Event Details: Selected event date, time slot, guest capacity, and customized requirements.
  • Location Data: Device GPS coordinates solely when you explicitly trigger "Near Me" venue searches.
  • Payment Information: Razorpay order IDs and transaction tokens. We never store credit/debit card numbers or UPI PINs on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is utilized solely to:

  • Deliver real-time venue search results and 3D architectural representations.
  • Transmit your booking requests directly to verified venue owners for mandatory manual review.
  • Generate legal tax invoices and Razorpay transaction records.
  • Send critical transactional notifications regarding approval status, payment confirmations, and reminder alerts.

3. Data Security & Storage

All data transmitted between your browser and our platform is encrypted using TLS 1.3 (256-bit SSL). Database persistence is secured via Supabase PostgreSQL featuring strict Row Level Security (RLS) policies, preventing unauthorized cross-user access or data leakage.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to access, edit, or delete your account information at any time directly through your Customer Dashboard or by contacting our data protection officer at privacy@hallkhoj.in.
