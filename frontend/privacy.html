Privacy Policy

Welcome to Hall Khoj Finder ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to safeguarding your personal privacy while providing transparent, world-class 3D venue discovery and booking services across India. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, process, protect, and delete your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary data required to facilitate marriage hall reservations, 3D tours, and verified transactions:

  • Contact & Profile Identifiers: Name, mobile number (+91), email address, and residential city entered during registration or reservation.
  • Event Reservation Parameters: Requested event date, slot (Morning / Evening / Full Day), estimated guest count, and custom notes for the venue owner.
  • Location Data: Device geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) captured strictly when you click "Use My Current Location" or "Near Me" to compute road distance to venues. We do not track background location.
  • Payment & Transaction Ledger: Razorpay payment identifiers, order numbers, and verified advance deposit amounts. We never store debit/credit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs on our servers.
  • Technical & Diagnostic Data: IP address, browser type, device category (Mobile/Tablet/Desktop), and operating system used strictly for fraud prevention and performance optimization.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly to fulfill legitimate marketplace functions:

  • To present available marriage halls, banquets, and event services within your requested locality and budget.
  • To transmit booking requests to verified venue owners for mandatory manual approval before any advance payment is requested.
  • To generate legally compliant vector PDF GST tax invoices and payment receipts.
  • To send critical transactional SMS and in-app alerts regarding approval, rejection, or confirmation.
  • To monitor application errors and resolve technical bugs reported through our engineering queue.

3. Data Storage, Security & Encryption

We implement industry-standard cybersecurity protections to ensure your information is never compromised:

  • Transport Security: All client-server communication is encrypted using 256-bit TLS 1.3 encryption (HTTPS enforced in production).
  • Database Protection: Data persistence is managed via Supabase PostgreSQL protected by Row Level Security (RLS) policies, ensuring customers and owners only access their authorized records.
  • Password Hashing: Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed using bcryptjs (10 rounds). Plaintext passwords are never saved.
  • Signature Verification: All payment callbacks are verified cryptographically using HMAC-SHA256 to prevent price tampering.

4. Third-Party Integrations

We work with trusted third-party providers who operate under strict confidentiality:

  • Razorpay: PCI-DSS certified payment gateway handling UPI, Card, and Netbanking transactions.
  • Supabase / PostgreSQL Cloud: Encrypted database and storage infrastructure.
  • Google Maps Platform / Leaflet OpenStreetMap: Map tiles and geocoding.
  • Google Gemini AI: Natural language query processing for grounded venue search.

5. Cookies & Tracking Preferences

We use essential cookies to maintain user authentication and booking state. Analytics and marketing cookies are activated strictly based on your preferences. You can update your choices anytime by clicking Cookie Settings in the footer.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion Requests

Under Indian data protection laws, you retain full sovereignty over your information:

  • Right to Access: View all your active bookings, past invoices, and profile data in your Customer Dashboard.
  • Right to Rectification: Edit phone, name, or preferences directly in your Profile settings.
  • Right to Erasure (Data Deletion): To permanently delete your account and personal records, submit a request via our Support Portal or email privacy@hallkhoj.in. Data will be purged within 7 business days, except records legally required for tax compliance.
