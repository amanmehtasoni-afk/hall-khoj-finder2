Our Mission & Vision

Reimagining How India Celebrates

From rural town marriage gardens to royal destination palaces, Hall Khoj Finder brings transparent pricing, 3D architectural walkthroughs, and guaranteed date availability to every family.

🎮

Interactive 3D Walkthroughs

Never book a venue blindly. Our Three.js 3D engine lets hosts inspect stage positions, table layouts, chandeliers and lighting atmospheres before visiting in person.

🛡️

Mandatory Owner Review

We never auto-confirm bookings without human verification. Venue owners review every date and event requirement personally, completely eliminating double bookings.

💳

Secure Advance Protection

Razorpay HMAC-SHA256 encrypted payments ensure token advances are held securely until the venue approval is finalized, with instantaneous GST tax invoices.

🇮🇳

14 Indian Languages

Bridging the digital divide across Hindi, Bhojpuri, Maithili, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu and 8 more languages so every family and local vendor can connect effortlessly.

Our Origin

Built by Event Engineers, for Indian Celebrations

Planning a wedding in India historically involved visiting dozens of marriage halls, haggling with brokers, dealing with unexpected double-bookings, and having zero visual preview of stage sizes or seating limits.

Hall Khoj Finder changed that forever. By combining WebGL 3D architectural visualization, real-time availability calendars, and strict server-side state machines, we give power back to families and verified local venue owners.
