1. Mandatory Manual Owner Approval Workflow

CORE BUSINESS PROTOCOL: Hall Khoj Finder operates strictly on an owner-reviewed reservation model. Submitting a reservation inquiry through the website does NOT constitute a confirmed booking.

  • When you submit a request, the booking enters the PENDING_OWNER_APPROVAL status.
  • Venue owners review the event date, expected guest count, and customized catering/decoration needs before accepting or declining.
  • Under no circumstances is advance payment accepted or processed prior to explicit venue owner approval.
  • If approved, the status transitions to APPROVED, unlocking the 25% advance token payment link. If declined, the status becomes REJECTED with an explanatory note.

2. 25% Advance Token Payment & Invoicing

To confirm an approved date reservation, hosts must submit the standard 25% advance deposit calculated automatically from verified database rates.

  • Payments are processed securely via Razorpay with cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 signature verification.
  • Upon successful verification, the booking status transitions to CONFIRMED, locking the venue calendar and preventing double bookings.
  • A legally binding GST tax invoice in vector PDF format is generated instantly and available in your Customer Dashboard.
  • The remaining 75% balance is payable directly to the venue owner pursuant to their individual contract terms.

3. Cancellation & Refund Policy

  • Cancellation by Host: Cancellations requested more than 30 days before the scheduled event date are eligible for a 70% refund of the advance token. Cancellations within 30 days are non-refundable unless otherwise agreed in writing with the venue owner.
  • Cancellation by Owner: In the rare event an owner cancels an approved booking due to force majeure or unforeseen licensing issues, the host receives a 100% full refund of all amounts paid.
  • Refunds are credited back to the original source method (UPI, Debit Card, Netbanking) within 5–7 business days.
