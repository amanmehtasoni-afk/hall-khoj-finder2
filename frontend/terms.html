1. Mandatory Manual Owner Approval Workflow

CRITICAL BUSINESS RULE: Hall Khoj Finder operates under a strict manual approval protocol. Submitting a booking request on this website does NOT constitute a confirmed booking.

  • All booking requests enter a PENDING_OWNER_APPROVAL state.

2. Advance Token Payments & Verification

All advance token payments are processed securely through Razorpay. Server-side HMAC-SHA256 signature verification is performed for every transaction before marking a booking as CONFIRMED. An official GST tax invoice is generated upon successful payment.
