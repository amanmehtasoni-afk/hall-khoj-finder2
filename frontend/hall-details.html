About The Venue

The Royal Grand Heritage Palace is a premier luxury destination for grand Indian weddings, royal receptions, engagements, and corporate events. The venue features an elevated royal wedding stage, separate vegetarian & non-vegetarian kitchen sections, and two complimentary bridal suites.
