# Hall Khoj Finder — Customer User Guide (Phase 7)

## 1. Finding Your Dream Venue
1. Open the Homepage (`/index.html`) or Search page (`/search.html`).
2. Use the **Transparent Advanced Search Bar** to select your State, District, City, Event Date, Guest Count, Venue Type, and Budget.
3. Click on any venue to open its details (`/hall-details.html?id=h-101`).

## 2. Inspecting in 3D
1. Click **"Inspect 3D Walkthrough"** or navigate to `/3d-preview.html`.
2. Rotate with left mouse drag, zoom with scroll wheel, and switch between **Daylight**, **Sunset**, and **Night Gala** lighting presets.
3. Inspect the elevated mandap stage, round banquet tables, and chandeliers.

## 3. Submitting a Booking Request
1. Click **"Book This Venue"** to open `/booking.html`.
2. Pick your event date, time slot, and guest count.
3. Submit the request. The initial status is set to `PENDING_OWNER_APPROVAL`.
4. The venue owner will review your dates manually.

## 4. Tracking and Advance Token Payment
1. View all requests under **My Bookings** (`/my-bookings.html`) or Customer Dashboard (`/dashboard.html`).
2. Once the owner approves, click **"Pay Advance Token"**.
3. Complete payment via Razorpay. Your dates are locked and your GST invoice pass is ready for download!
