Abstract Hall Khoj Finder is an enterprise-ready, full-stack event services marketplace engineered specifically for the unorganized Indian celebration ecosystem. In India, organizing weddings, receptions, and corporate galas requires booking disparate vendors—including banquet halls, wedding cars, tent houses, caterers, DJs, sound systems, and photographers—a process historically plagued by extreme market fragmentation, opaque offline pricing, 15% to 30% middleman broker markups, and exhausting physical site inspections. Hall Khoj Finder resolves these foundational challenges by unifying 19 distinct event service categories into a single, high-performance web platform. The platform is built using a modern full-stack architecture comprising HTML5, CSS3 with glassmorphism, vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), Three.js (r128), Node.js, Express.js (v5.2.1), and a resilient hybrid database layer featuring MongoDB Atlas with Mongoose ODM alongside an automatic transactional JSON failover engine. A standout technical innovation is the interactive 3D procedural venue walkthrough powered by Three.js, which enables prospective clients to examine architectural proportions, stage positioning, chandeliers, banquet seating, and dining areas under three dynamic lighting ambiances (Daylight, Sunset, and Night Gala) directly inside standard web browsers. Furthermore, the system incorporates a conflict-protected booking state machine that enforces atomic date-level locks, a server-side 25% advance token payment pipeline integrated with Razorpay and verified via cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 signatures, a natural language AI venue search engine supporting colloquial Hindi, Hinglish, and English without hallucinations, and a Pan-India localization engine covering 14 Indian languages. By empowering customers with transparent pricing and equipping venue operators with comprehensive administrative SaaS dashboards, Hall Khoj Finder delivers a secure, accessible, and mathematically verifiable digital solution for modern event planning. 2. PAGE 2 — INTRODUCTION &amp; PROBLEM STATEMENT 3. Introduction The Indian wedding and celebration industry represents an economic powerhouse exceeding INR 4,00,000+ Crore ($50 Billion USD) annually. Across metropolitan cities and tier-2/tier-3 districts alike, family events represent pivotal social milestones requiring substantial financial investments and coordination across dozens of independent suppliers. Despite widespread digital transformation in retail, food delivery, and travel, the event venue and allied hospitality sector remains deeply rooted in informal, unorganized, and paper-based practices. Finding a suitable banquet hall or tent vendor still entails word-of-mouth inquiries, physically traversing multiple venues across city traffic, negotiating ambiguous pricing with unregistered middlemen, and recording dates in physical paper diaries. Hall Khoj Finder has been engineered to modernize this vital sector through a robust, mobile-first web marketplace. The platform bridges the divide between venue hosts and prospective clients by digitizing catalog discovery, spatial inspection, date-level availability tracking, financial advances, and customer reputation management. By providing direct, commission-free communication and automated business tooling, the application democratizes market access for micro-vendors while guaranteeing financial and operational peace of mind for celebrating families. 4. Problem Statement The development of Hall Khoj Finder is motivated by five systemic breakdowns observed in the conventional event management ecosystem: • Severe Market Fragmentation: Couples and families must separately seek out marriage halls, luxury wedding cars, tent houses, caterers, and decorators across disjointed classifieds, causing extreme logistical friction. • Remote Spatial Visualization Gap: Static 2D photographs posted on social media fail to convey true spatial proportions, stage dimensions, pillar placements, or ceiling heights, compelling users to travel physically just to assess room capacity. • Exploitative Brokerage Networks: Unregulated local middlemen often inflate hall rental rates by 15% to 30%, exploiting urgent customer demand during peak auspicious wedding dates (muhurats). • Concurrency Failures &amp; Double-Booking: Manual paper registers and uncoordinated telephone inquiries frequently lead to accidental double-booking of high-demand dates, resulting in catastrophic last-minute cancellations. • Opaque Pricing &amp; Insecure Cash Advances: Vendors demand substantial untracked cash advances without legally enforceable digital receipts, formal cancellation policies, or payment reconciliation. 5. Motivation The primary motivation behind this project is to apply modern web technologies—specifically hardware-accelerated WebGL computer graphics, event-driven REST APIs, cryptographic verification, and generative artificial intelligence—to an economically critical yet technically underserved domain. Bridging high-stakes consumer transactions with verified vendor identities creates an equitable ecosystem where small businesses thrive and consumers are protected from exploitation. 6. Existing System Analysis The existing event discovery and venue booking system in India operates almost exclusively through traditional offline mechanisms. Families seeking a banquet hall or marriage garden must compile phone numbers from local directories or friends, physically visit venues scattered across suburban localities, and negotiate rates directly with on-site caretakers. Venue owners are furnished with a multi-functional SaaS portal for managing listings, blocking dates, and tracking revenue metrics, while platform administrators oversee KYC compliance, listings moderation, and security audit trails. 9. System Architecture Hall Khoj Finder implements an enterprise-grade multi-tier architecture designed for high availability, security, and low latency: • Client Presentation Tier: Standard web browsers render semantic HTML5, CSS3 glassmorphism, vanilla JavaScript ES6+ modules, Three.js WebGL context, and Leaflet map canvases. • Security &amp; Middleware Tier: Express middleware intercepting incoming requests to enforce Helmet security headers, CSP, sliding-window rate limiting, JWT validation, and deep recursive input sanitization against XSS and NoSQL injection. • Application &amp; Controller Tier: Modular Express 5 REST controllers routing traffic to dedicated services: Booking State Machine, Payment Service, AI Natural Language Parser, and SEO Generator. • Persistence &amp; Data Storage Tier: Dual-storage database architecture featuring MongoDB Atlas via Mongoose ODM as the primary data store, with an automatic transactional JSON failover layer ensuring uninterrupted operation during local development or network disconnections. 18. Data Flow Architecture Data moves through the system following a strictly validated lifecycle: A client request initiates at the frontend and is transmitted via HTTPS as a JSON payload accompanied by a Bearer JWT. The request passes through the rate-limiting and input-sanitizing middlewares. The relevant controller authenticates user identity and role via RBAC, validates request schemas, and invokes the appropriate service layer. For booking operations, the Booking Service queries the database to verify date availability. If available, a 25% advance token order is generated. Upon successful Razorpay payment, cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 signatures are validated on the backend. The booking status is atomically transitioned to CONFIRMED, the calendar date is locked, and real-time notification alerts are dispatched. 19. PAGE 7 — SECURITY, TESTING &amp; REQUIREMENTS 21. Security Architecture &amp; Cryptographic Controls Hall Khoj Finder enforces seven verifiable security controls directly implemented in the codebase: • 1. Stateless JWT Authentication: RFC 7519 JSON Web Tokens signed with a 256-bit secret key authenticate API requests, eliminating server-side session overhead. • 2. Cryptographic Password Hashing: User and owner passwords are one-way hashed using Bcrypt.js with automated salting, preventing plain-text credential leaks. • 3. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Strict middleware validates user roles (customer, owner, staff, admin) on every protected endpoint, immediately rejecting unauthorized requests with 403 Forbidden. • 4. HMAC-SHA256 Signature Verification: Payment settlement requires timing-safe cryptographic verification (crypto.timingSafeEqual) of Razorpay signatures, preventing client-side price manipulation. • 5. In-Memory Sliding-Window Rate Limiting: A custom rate limiter restricts traffic (300 req/min globally; 100 req/min for auth, AI, and payments) to safeguard against DDoS and brute-force attacks. • 6. Deep Recursive Input Sanitization: All request payloads are recursively stripped of HTML &lt;script&gt; tags, javascript: protocols, and NoSQL injection operators. • 7. Helmet-Grade HTTP Security Headers: Enforces Content-Security-Policy (CSP), Strict-Transport-Security (HSTS), X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, and X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN. 22.
